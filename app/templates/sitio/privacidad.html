What data we collect and why

When you write to us or ask for a quote

Your name, company, email, phone, the message you send and the page you came from. We use them to reply and to prepare a proposal. Legal basis: your request and the steps before a possible contract.

When you take the free assessment

Your website address, your answers, your name, company and email. We analyse your website with public information (the page itself, Google's speed test and your public Google business listing) and email you the result. Legal basis: your consent, which you give by ticking the box.

If you ask to receive ideas and case studies

Only if you tick that box, we'll email you now and then. You can unsubscribe from the link in any email or by writing to us. Legal basis: your consent.

When you browse the site

We measure which pages are visited and which buttons are clicked, to understand what works. We don't use this to identify you. See Cookies.

If you're a client and we run the voice assistant or other automations with your customers' data, that processing is governed by the data processing agreement we sign with you.

Who we share it with

We don't sell your data or give it to third parties for their own purposes. We use these providers, who process it only on our behalf:

  • Railway: hosting for the website and the database.
  • Brevo (servers in the EU): contact list and sending of the assessment and newsletters.
  • Resend: sending of confirmation emails and internal notices about new requests.
  • PostHog (servers in the EU): website analytics.
  • Google: speed test and business listing lookup for the assessment. Only your website address is sent, not your personal data.

Some of these providers are based in the United States. In that case the transfer is covered by the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep it

Requests and assessments: up to two years after our last contact if you don't become a client. If you do, for as long as we work together and then for the periods required by law. Newsletters: until you unsubscribe.

Your rights

You can ask to access, correct or delete your data, object to or restrict its use, take it with you, or withdraw your consent at any time. Write to {{ owner.email }} and we'll reply within a month. If you think we haven't handled your data properly, you can complain to the Spanish Data Protection Agency (aepd.es).

This site isn't aimed at children under 14.

Cookies

We don't use advertising cookies. We use one analytics cookie from PostHog (ph_…_posthog, lasts one year) that counts visits and clicks with a random identifier. You can delete it or block it in your browser settings and the site will keep working.
